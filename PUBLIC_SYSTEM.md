# Whole Donuts Universe public system

`https://wenevergonnaclose.com/` is the single public entry for Whole Donuts
Universe. This document is the source-of-truth map for what the public site
offers, what it does not offer, and how a maintainer may consider new public
material.

## Public paths

| Path | Available now | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Community start-here | Yes. The Community Counter, Whole Donuts path, and The Nurtured Chef path are public and optional. | No account, profile, enrollment, or personal-data requirement. |
| Browser-local welcome and +U return link | Yes. Optional choices and the return link remain in the visitor's browser. | They are not identities, credentials, profiles, analytics, or server records. |
| +U Library | Yes. The tracked templates may be copied and adapted for personal use. | Do not add private, sensitive, or third-party material without permission. |
| +U World | Yes. The visual studio is an optional public exploration path. | It does not create an account or collect a contribution. |
| Donuts New School and Ambassador direction | Yes. They are open learning and leadership directions. | They are not enrollment, certification, employment, an application, or a guaranteed opportunity. |
| Reviewed crumb process | Preparation only. The protocol and template are public. | The static site has no submission runtime; publication is always human-reviewed. |
| Browser-only review queue | Yes. It renders curated metadata already shipped with the site. | It cannot scan, ingest, publish, merge, or access repositories or networks. |
| Goods Window | Browse-only. | The +U storefront is unavailable until an owner configures a verified HTTPS URL. |
| Whole Donuts and TNC stores | External links only. | Availability, transactions, terms, fulfillment, and privacy are set by the external provider. |
| Optional support | A deliberate external Cash App exit or manual Chime instruction is available after confirmation. | It is separate from merchandise and never creates an entitlement, reward, or public record. |

## Privacy and safety boundary

Visitor interactions are browser-local by default. The public bundle must not
collect or request names, email addresses, faces or images, exact age or birth
date, location, sensitive data, repository source contents, secrets, or
operational configuration.

Do not add analytics, tracking, accounts, profiling, network scanning,
automatic content intake, automatic publication, automatic GitHub actions, or
automatic merges. Do not add external assets or dependencies. A future
capability is not public until it is explicitly implemented, reviewed, and
truthfully labeled.

## Human-reviewed source curation

Community learning material may come only from explicitly submitted original
work or permitted public contributions. A contribution must be voluntary and
sent through an owner-approved reviewed channel; the public static site is not
that channel.

Before a maintainer adds material to a public page, template, or queue, they
must:

1. Confirm the item is original or has explicit, adequate permission for the
   intended public reuse.
2. Record a minimal source reference, provenance, rights status, review status,
   attribution requirement, and the manual next step.
3. Review privacy, safety, accuracy for the stated use, accessibility, and
   usefulness. Remove or decline material that includes private, sensitive,
   operational, deceptive, harmful, or unclear-rights content.
4. Keep Sunshine templates and protocol references metadata-only with `needs
   owner review` until explicit reuse rights are documented.
5. Keep WHNutz material, merch material, operational documents, repository
   contents, secrets, and configuration out of public-source candidates unless
   a separate explicit rights and safety decision makes a sanitized derivative
   eligible.
6. Make the proposed change locally, complete human repository review, and
   merge only after approval. Publication requires a separate manual Pages
   dispatch by a maintainer.

The review queue is a shortlist for this process, not a feed. It must never
discover sources, read a repository, scan a network, create a submission,
change a source, publish material, or merge a change automatically.

## Maintainer release checklist

For an eligible public addition, update the relevant public page or template,
`review-queue-manifest.js` when appropriate, this system map when availability
changes, and the release notes. Verify the Pages artifact stages only intended
public files and preserves `CNAME` exactly as `wenevergonnaclose.com`. Dispatch
the deployment manually only after the reviewed merge is present on `main`.

The +U idea

A problem can become a situation. A situation can become a useful next step. The phrase U + U = 3 is a metaphor: two people can create a third thing together - a relationship, a shared effort, or a movement. It is an invitation to participate, not a promise of a particular outcome.

How a future direction becomes public

  1. An owner verifies the destination's domain control, current content, and intended role.
  2. A human reviews rights, privacy, safety, accessibility, consumer terms where relevant, and a visible return path to wenevergonnaclose.com.
  3. The canonical site adds a truthful, clearly labeled link only after that review is complete.

A planned domain is not a public capability. Until a destination passes this process, the canonical gateway stays focused on the present on-site experience and every public route remains optional and non-blocking.

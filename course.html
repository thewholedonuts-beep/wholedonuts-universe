Start small. Carry something useful forward.

This self-directed course turns a useful idea into one next step, one shareable lesson, or one table-building plan. It is free to use and print. It creates no account, does not store responses, and does not submit anything.

  2. Print the full course or one worksheet.

MODULE 1 · FIND YOUR NEXT STEP

Turn one idea into one doable action.

Choose an action that can happen in less than fifteen minutes. Keep the page about what is useful now, not what has to be perfect.

What brought me here?
Write one sentence about what you want support with, learn, make, or share.
One small next step
Choose one action that takes less than fifteen minutes.
What would make it easier?
Name one person, tool, place, or piece of information that could help.
What I can leave for the next person
Share one useful note, connection, or encouragement when you are ready.

Free to use, adapt, and share. Do not put private, financial, health, or identifying information on a public copy.

MODULE 2 · SHARE WHAT HELPED

Make one lesson easier to carry forward.

A crumb is a useful lesson, connection, tool, place, or practice. This worksheet helps you prepare it; this site does not accept or publish submissions.

Something that helped me
Name one idea, person, tool, place, or practice that made a difference.
When it might help someone else
Describe the situation in plain language. Keep private details out.
How to start
Offer one small, realistic first action.
What to remember
Add a kind note you wish someone had given you.

Share only original or permitted material through an owner-approved reviewed channel. Read the Crumb Protocol before submitting anything when a reviewed channel becomes available.

MODULE 3 · BUILD YOUR TABLE

Gather around one useful first action.

A table can be a small circle, a project, or a practical act of care. Keep it voluntary, useful, and open to the perspectives that can help.

The reason we are gathering
Describe the cause, project, or practical need in one sentence.
Who belongs at this table?
List the skills, lived experience, resources, or perspectives that would help.
The first shared action
Choose one action that can happen this week.
How we will keep the door open
Decide how people can contribute time, skills, materials, connections, or voluntary support.

This course does not solicit donations or create a contribution record. Optional support is separate and always a deliberate provider choice.

CONTRIBUTOR IMPACT

Make substantial support understandable.

A single verified voluntary-support payment of $250 or more enters the contributor impact path. The contributor receives a private report describing what the support enabled, where it was applied, and the next measurable outcome.

01

Verify

Confirm that funds were received and identify the intended initiative without treating a payment screenshot or message as proof.

02

Report privately

Deliver impact information directly to the contributor by default. Keep personal and payment information out of the public experience.

03

Recognize by consent

Publish a name, organization, story, or contribution only after separate, explicit consent that can be declined without affecting the private impact report.

Current activation status. The public site does not yet receive payment-provider verification or securely collect contributor contact and recognition consent. Until that integration is live, this path requires manual verification and private follow-up. Support never purchases partnership approval, authority, rewards, or public endorsement.

PLAIN AND CLEAR

Professional trust is earned before a public claim.

  • No organization is represented until authority is independently verified.
  • A prior purchase or voluntary support payment never creates partnership authority, sponsorship, approval, or preferential review.
  • Verified support of $250 or more receives private impact reporting by default; public recognition requires separate explicit consent.
  • No official name, badge, emblem, uniform mark, story, or design is used without permission.
  • No purchased contact lists, hidden tracking, automatic messages, or guaranteed viral claims.
  • Voluntary support, store purchases, sponsorships, and rewards remain separate.
  • Whole Donuts is an LLC, not a nonprofit, and provides no charitable tax receipt.
  • The public workspace collects no form response, identity document, student record, payment detail, or confidential board material.

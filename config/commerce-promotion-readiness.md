# Commerce and promotion readiness policy

This is a non-deployed policy for decisions that must remain access-controlled
until a responsible entity authorizes them. It creates no price, discount,
product, vendor, checkout, account, customer-data flow, or public claim. The
Pages workflow does not publish `config/`.

## Future 15% pricing-uplift proposal

A future **15% pricing uplift** is a proposal, not a current price change or
calculation. It may be considered for one approved product only after all of
the following are documented and human-approved:

1. Verified base cost for that product and its approved variant.
2. Applicable taxes, shipping, fees, and merchant decision.
3. Provider mapping and fulfillment capability for the approved product.
4. A consumer-facing displayed price and all required disclosures.

The percentage alone does not authorize a new price, displayed price, product
listing, or claim that any price has increased.

## Separate classifications

| Classification | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Optional support contribution | Voluntary support only. It creates no purchase, discount, reward, tax-deductibility promise, referral credit, or benefit entitlement. |
| Merchandise purchase | A future merchant checkout transaction that may carry a separate, published purchase promotion under its own terms. |
| Movement recognition | Non-financial acknowledgement. It is not payment-based and does not confer a discount, product, cash value, or benefit entitlement. |

Do not call optional support a discount-eligibility condition or use
“donation” to describe a purchase-promotion qualification. Cash App and Chime
remain optional support only, not merchandise payment or checkout.

## Future purchase-promotion requirements

A future discount may exist only as a product- and merchant-specific purchase
promotion after human approval. Before public release, publish applicable terms
that cover:

- the specific product or eligible products;
- merchant identity and approval;
- start and end time;
- eligibility and exclusions;
- displayed price and price-before/price-after disclosure where required;
- redemption, reconciliation, and customer-support process; and
- privacy-minimizing handling of required checkout data.

The promotion must not be a donor reward, referral benefit, invitation credit,
or movement-recognition entitlement.

## Identity and data boundary

Movement or member identity remains outside this static site. Do not introduce
a personal QR, profile, invitation credit, scan tracking, account, or benefit
entitlement unless a separate secure, consented member architecture and final
terms are approved. The ecosystem site must not collect, upload, or retain
customer data or order exports; a future approved merchant checkout handles
only its necessary data under its own terms.

## Human go/no-go checklist

Do not approve a public commerce or promotion release until each applicable
item has a documented human decision:

- responsible entity and merchant of record;
- approved product, source artwork, rights, brand use, and print proof;
- provider configuration and verified product/fulfillment mapping;
- taxes, refunds, shipping, packaging, support, and privacy responsibilities;
- accurate consumer price and promotion disclosures;
- published promotion terms and redemption/reconciliation process; and
- final release authority, rollback contact, and pause procedure.

No vendor is selected by this policy. The separate
[print-asset intake manifest](print-asset-intake-manifest.md) remains the
required evidence path for artwork and proof readiness.

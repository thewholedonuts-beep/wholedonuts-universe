# Print-asset recovery and approval manifest

This internal, non-deployed manifest defines how an owner-supplied or recovered
candidate becomes eligible for a future print proof. It contains no asset,
provider credential, product ID, customer data, or vendor activation. The Pages
workflow does not publish `config/`.

## Current recovery status

No print-ready logo, image, print file, print template, product identifier, or
product-specific rights record was found in the reachable Universe, WHNutz,
merch-platform, or Sunshine histories. Every future candidate is therefore
**awaiting owner-provided import**.

The owner-directed **+U Neck Line** is a new planned neckline or
neck-placement expression, not a recovered original item or necklace/neckwear
product. It remains awaiting owner-provided artwork and must complete this same
intake, rights, brand-approval, placement, and human print-proof process.

The recovered filenames `MovementTrifoldOUTSIDE.png` and
`MovementTrifoldINSIDE.png` are missing original brochure references, not
recovered merchandise artwork or approved print assets. Do not repurpose,
recreate, or call either print-ready without the original file and separate
rights/brand review.

## Candidate record

Create one private candidate record per supplied file. Do not add the file to a
public artifact until every required approval is complete.

| Required field | Record |
|---|---|
| Candidate status | Awaiting owner-provided import, intake review, rights review, proof review, approved, paused, or rejected. |
| Original source | Original location and supplying owner or rights holder. |
| Version | Content hash and source revision or supplied-file version. |
| Technical description | Original filename, file format, pixel dimensions, color profile, and any production constraints. |
| Rights and brand approval | Documented human approval for the exact asset, mark/expression, intended use, and any required attribution. |
| Placement compatibility | Proposed placement/design direction and compatible concept only; do not infer garment, print-area, or provider support. |
| Proof result | Human proof reviewer, review date, result, and any approved revision. |
| Release decision | Human approver, public revision, and rollback or pause reference. |

Never substitute a screenshot, mockup, generated reconstruction, or unverified
copy for an original print asset.

## Intake and proof sequence

1. Receive the original asset from its owner or an authorized source through an
   approved private channel.
2. Record the source, owner, hash/version, format, dimensions, and color
   profile without publishing private metadata.
3. Verify rights, brand approval, attribution requirements, and the intended
   placement before any rendering or vendor discussion.
4. Confirm that the asset and placement fit the proposed future concept without
   claiming a product or provider capability.
5. Obtain a human-reviewed print proof and record its result.
6. Make any provider, catalog, checkout, or public availability decision in a
   separate reviewed change.

## Later vendor evaluation criteria

When a separate activation decision is authorized, compare candidates without
making a recommendation in this document: sample and print-proof quality;
relevant product and print-area support; packaging; production and shipping
geography and timing; taxes, returns, and customer support; customer-data
practices; and total cost. Verify claims directly with the proposed provider at
that time.

No provider is selected here. Do not connect Printify, Printful, Shopify, or
any other service; create product IDs; upload assets; send customer data; or
enable checkout based on this manifest.

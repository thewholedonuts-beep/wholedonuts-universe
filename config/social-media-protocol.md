# Social Media Protocol

This operating guidance is not deployed in the GitHub Pages artifact. Future
platform accounts, credentials, provider configuration, and moderation records
belong in access-controlled systems. Do not add account connections, API
clients, keys, embeds, analytics SDKs, or actual accounts to this repository.

## Entry and destination

Use `https://wenevergonnaclose.com/` as the canonical ecosystem entry. Before
publishing, a human selects the relevant subject or brand destination rather
than treating the shared +U entry as a replacement for distinct brands. Use a
plain-language link that identifies the destination.

For the free +U course, use only the direct canonical course URL:
`https://wenevergonnaclose.com/course.html`. Do not use tracking parameters,
shortened links, link-in-bio services, or unverified companion domains.

## Manual launch readiness

Before a responsible human creates or publishes from a platform account:

1. Confirm that the canonical root and course URL are live, readable without an
   account, and match the approved public content.
2. Confirm that the post names the Whole Donuts Universe destination plainly
   and does not claim a store, product, account, certification, enrollment,
   submission channel, reward, donation benefit, or companion capability is
   live.
3. Use text-only publishing unless a separate image, video, logo, or other
   media asset has documented rights, provenance, accessibility text, and human
   approval. Do not reuse companion-repository material by implication.
4. Publish manually from the responsible account. Keep native platform
   analytics, messages, comments, credentials, moderation records, and
   platform settings out of this repository.

Approved course launch copy, subject to the responsible human's platform and
timing review:

> The free +U course has print-ready worksheets for choosing one next step,
> sharing what helped, and building a table. No account or submission is
> required: https://wenevergonnaclose.com/course.html

The social preview metadata on the course page supports this direct link. It
does not create or manage a social account, publish a post, add a tracker, or
guarantee preview, reach, indexing, or platform availability.

## Approval and contribution review

No content is published until a responsible human approves the platform,
destination, copy, media, and timing. Community material requires voluntary
consent plus confirmation of source, rights, and attribution before review.
Apply the Learning Model's source, rights, safety, usefulness, privacy, and
fit review before publication.

## Accessible and safe communication

Provide useful alt text for images; captions and transcripts for applicable
audio or video; and plain-language links and calls to action. Follow community
safety and moderation practices: review reported harm, correct material when
needed, escalate safety or legal concerns to the responsible entity, and avoid
amplifying unsafe, exploitative, or unverified material.

Do not scrape identities, ingest direct-message data, track recipients or
referrals, enrich profiles, or cross-post automatically. Do not require a
person to share, follow, or provide personal information to access the public
entry, participate, or receive recognition.

## Labels, provenance, and corrections

Label optional support, external purchases, and general information clearly.
Do not call purchases donations or represent support as a payment, investment,
currency, or guaranteed benefit. Retain the source, approval, attribution,
revision history, and publication context for approved material. Provide a
human-reviewed removal or correction path and record the decision without
automatically deleting source material.

## No-auto rule

No automation may post, cross-post, import private data, process payment,
allocate support, grant rewards, create donation records, or delete material.
The Share & Invitation Protocol creates only a user-initiated invitation link;
it does not connect a social account, send a message, or create a referral,
recipient, payment, donor, or reward record.

# Consolidation and learning model

This is the operating model for making Whole Donuts Universe the verified
system of record for ecosystem material. It does not claim that every legacy
repository, reserved domain, or private owner file has been migrated. Keep
private registry details, credentials, and owner files in access-controlled
systems; this document is excluded from the Pages artifact.

## Intake and classification

Classify each candidate item before importing it:

| Category | Handling |
|---|---|
| Public entry | Review, preserve provenance, and add to the public Pages artifact only when ready. |
| Brand content | Assign to its distinct subject or domain; do not collapse brand identity into the shared entry. |
| Learning asset | Review source, rights, safety, and relevance before publishing through an intentional channel. |
| Operational config | Keep in non-public operations paths and never include it in the Pages artifact. |
| Secret or private | Do not import into the repository or deployment artifact. Keep access-controlled. |
| Generated or disposable | Recreate when needed; do not treat it as source material. |
| Archive-only | Preserve provenance and retain outside the active public system until a reviewed use exists. |

## Preserve before remove

Before replacing, redirecting, archiving, or retiring a legacy item, record its
source location, revision or retrieval date, owner or provenance, classification,
destination, and verification result. Confirm the destination works, rights and
attribution are reviewed, required links or redirects are approved, and no
secret, private, generated, or disposable material crossed into the public
artifact. Archive or redirect only after those checks; never delete as a
migration shortcut.

## Learning loop

1. Accept lessons and contributions only through intentional reviewed channels.
2. Validate source, attribution, rights, safety, usefulness, and the relevant subject before publication.
3. Publish approved material to the appropriate community subject or domain, retaining source and revision history.
4. Measure feedback and impact to inform a human review of the next revision.
5. Keep rejected, deferred, and archive-only material out of automatic publication paths.

No automation may publish material, delete source material, import credentials or
private data, initiate payment, transfer funds, allocate value, auto-pay,
auto-reward, or grant rewards.

## Anonymous Dashboard

The public +U Dashboard may store only a minimal local accessory list after a
person voluntarily completes the welcome flow. It must not collect or create a
name, face, photo, biometric, contact detail, public profile, server record,
unique identifier, payment record, recipient record, or referral record.
Accessories can reflect only voluntary non-financial exploration and must never
depend on support selection, payment, purchase, sharing recipients, referrals,
or external tracking. A reset removes the local Dashboard record and restarts
the welcome flow. A +U pass is a device-local return link, not an identity.

## Staged legacy retirement

Keep legacy repositories available while their verified replacement is tested.
After the public artifact, provenance, and approved redirects are confirmed,
archive or redirect the legacy surface as appropriate. Do not delete repositories,
domains, deployments, or unclassified material as part of consolidation.

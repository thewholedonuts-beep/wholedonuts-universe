# SEO Crumb Saver operating model

This non-deployed protocol guides human-reviewed organic discovery. It is not
an analytics service, submission backend, visitor profile, rank-manipulation
tool, or automatic publishing system.

## Public discovery

The canonical entry is `https://wenevergonnaclose.com/`. Deliberate public
pages, guides, templates, and voluntary canonical invitations may help people
discover useful reviewed knowledge. A crumb is the reviewed content itself, not
a person, search history, referral, direct message, social profile, or payment
signal.

## Human review loop

1. A responsible person manually reviews aggregate Search Console or Bing
   Webmaster visibility and public index status outside this repository.
2. They identify a useful public-content gap without collecting identity data.
3. They verify rights, source, safety, accessibility, accuracy, and subject fit.
4. They manually approve and publish a useful improvement with attribution and
   revision history when appropriate.
5. They review public feedback and visibility later; neither automatically
   changes content, rewards, payments, Dashboard unlocks, nor Launch Window counts.

Daily target evaluation is a separate human-reviewed operation. It must not
use a `?u=` return link, visitor interaction, payment, social activity, or
Fresh Launch state as a target, activation signal, or outcome.

## Prohibitions

Do not scrape identities, direct messages, private search data, personal
profiles, or third-party content without permission. Do not automatically
publish, cross-post, delete, manipulate rankings, enroll a person, send
submissions, or use SEO data for payment, reward, donation, or identity
decisions. Do not add Search Console, Bing, analytics, API clients, trackers,
beacons, credentials, or keys to this repository or public artifact.

## Submission capability

The current static public site has no configured Crumb Saver submission runtime.
Keep its form visibly unavailable so it cannot accept or discard text. Offer
only real preparation actions: guides, templates, the reviewed protocol,
canonical invitation sharing, and public contact. A separate endpoint may be
linked only after its owner supplies and verifies it.

# Donation Access Hub operating model

This operational guidance is not deployed in the GitHub Pages artifact. Keep
entity-specific accounting records, provider configuration, and eligibility
decisions in an access-controlled system.

## Scope

The Donation Access Hub is an interface and policy layer. It is not a payment
processor, bank, wallet, fund-transfer system, recurring-billing system, or
automatic allocation system. It must not store payment methods or initiate
payments, transfers, allocations, or rewards without a person's deliberate
action.

## Required public flow

1. A person chooses an optional support purpose.
2. The page identifies the processor and payee before any exit.
3. The person affirms a final action before an external processor link appears.
4. The external provider handles the payment experience under its own terms.

Optional support contributions are not store purchases. Store product,
fulfillment, refund, tax, and merchant-of-record terms must remain with the
separate store provider.

## Provider availability

The provider set is Cash App and Chime only. Cash App to `$wholedonuts` is the
only currently configured optional support exit and must remain behind the
purpose, disclosure, and final-action gate. Chime is a future manual provider;
do not create a Chime link, sign, placeholder, or payable destination until a
verified public destination is supplied. Do not add providers or account
language outside this set. Provider selection must not identify a person or
initiate a payment or transfer.

## Impact stewardship

Show the selected support purpose before a person leaves for a provider. Do not
allocate support automatically. Publish impact claims or updates only after
responsible human review and accounting. Do not promise a particular use, tax
treatment, or outcome for every dollar.

## Recognition and rewards

Recognition or access must not be cash, currency, ownership, an investment, a
prize, a gift card, or a guaranteed benefit. It must never be required in
exchange for, allocated from, or contingent upon a charitable donation or
optional support payment. An intentional reviewed contribution or share may be
recognized only under future published eligibility rules; it is never automatic.

## Sharing and invitations

The invitation link contains no recipient, referral, donor, or personal data.
Use native sharing only after a person explicitly activates it. A share does not
create a payment, referral credit, record, donor entitlement, or automatic reward.

## Responsible-entity decisions

The responsible entity and configured providers must determine accounting,
refund handling, merchant-of-record status, charitable eligibility, tax
receipts, and reward eligibility before representing any of those matters
publicly. Do not imply tax deductibility, investment return, financial value,
or guaranteed benefits unless the responsible entity has made and documented
that determination.

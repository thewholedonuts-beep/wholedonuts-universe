This is an internal, non-deployed evaluation policy. It selects no vendor,
affiliate, sponsor, product, price, checkout, customer-data flow, or public
link. The Pages workflow does not publish `config/`.

## Relationship gates

| Relationship | Required state sequence | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor | `candidate` -> `evaluated` -> `approved vendor` -> `contracted provider` | Advance only by documented human review and the commerce go/no-go checklist. |
| Affiliate | `affiliate candidate` -> `approved affiliate relationship` -> `disclosed affiliate link` | A qualifying link may appear only after written approval and an adjacent, plain-language financial-relationship disclosure. |
| Sponsor | `sponsor candidate` -> `written sponsorship agreement` -> `approved public sponsor acknowledgment` | Public acknowledgment requires explicit written approval, scope and term, brand-usage rights, and a truthful visible disclosure. |

An affiliate, vendor, purchaser, referral, or completed order never
automatically becomes a sponsor. No QR, invitation, customer, affiliate,
vendor, referral, order, or payment information may enter candidate evaluation
or change a relationship state.

## Catalog, promotion, and privacy gate

The [future concept catalog](merch-concept-catalog.md) remains **not for sale**
until an approved vendor/product mapping and every item in the
[commerce go/no-go checklist](commerce-promotion-readiness.md) are approved.
This policy does not authorize an affiliate/referral link, vendor integration,
account, customer-data transfer, checkout, promotion, payment, or public UI.

Any future affiliate disclosure must be adjacent to each qualifying link or
content and plainly explain the financial relationship. It must not rely on a
profile, scan, invitation credit, or hidden tracking. Optional support,
movement recognition, and purchases remain separate under the commerce policy.

### Context safeguards

- **Schools and young people:** obtain institution approval, use age-appropriate
  materials, and do not collect student or minor data through a public template.
- **9/11 or other remembrance:** center memory, service, learning, and care. Do not
  imply affiliation, exploit loss, or connect remembrance automatically to sales
  or rewards.
- **Football or sports:** use generic community-sports language unless written
  permission covers league, team, player, mascot, uniform, or event marks.
- **New-year campaigns:** state the real dates and goals; do not promise guaranteed
  transformation.
